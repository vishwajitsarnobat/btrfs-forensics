# EXP-007 — Reading a pre-balance state through the chunk map of its own time

- **Date:** 2026-09-21 (registration)
- **Milestone:** M5a, and M5's definition of done ("`s01` yields a reconstructed historical chunk
  map and correctly-translated outside-map orphans"); claim C6
- **Script:** [`experiments/exp007.py`](exp007.py); environment: [`experiments/env.sh`](env.sh)
- **Raw outputs:** `images/scratch/exp/EXP-007/results.jsonl` (gitignored), one line per build
- **Registration:** §1 and §2 were committed before any historical map was built or any file was
  read through one. **What was known beforehand**, so the reader can judge how blind this is:
  - M4b's result on `s01_discard_none_r1`: from a pre-balance state, 3 files come out `partial`,
    every extent `unmapped` (catalog.md, M4b);
  - M2b's counts of blocks that a state's own chunk items place (`states.maps_historical`,
    EXP-002: 26 of 35 states have a chunk root that differs from the current one);
  - a listing, made today while designing M5a, of the CHUNK_ITEMs and DEV_EXTENTs in the scanned
    leaves of `s01_discard_none_r1`: chunk-tree leaves of generations 2 to 6, 17, 23, 24, 29, 30
    and 38 survive, and the old data chunk (logical 13631488) is in every one of them before 38.
  No translation of any block or extent through a historical map had been computed.

## 1. Hypothesis

After a full balance, the chunk maps of the time before it are still on the disk, and reading
through them gives back what the current map cannot address.

- **H1 (file data, no discard).** On a build without discard, every regular file of a pre-balance
  state that the current map leaves `partial` with `unmapped` extents comes out `complete` when
  read through the state's own map, and every such file the scenario's log names (`keep.txt`,
  `deleted_big.txt`) has the logged SHA-256. Registered: all of them, in 5 of 5 builds; no
  `complete` file with a wrong hash, ever. The reason to expect it: a balance copies the data and
  frees the old chunk without writing to it, and the three new chunks are allocated at higher
  device offsets than the old data chunk.
- **H2 (discard).** With `discard=async` and the quick unmount of this scenario, the result equals
  H1 (EXP-002 found every stale metadata block still in place; the same is expected of the old
  data chunk). This is the less certain prediction: nothing measured so far says what happens to
  a freed *data* chunk under async discard. With `discard=sync` the historical maps gain nothing:
  EXP-002 found only 2 states there, both after the balance began, and the freed chunks are
  trimmed. Registered: async as H1; sync 0 files gained, and no file that is `complete` with a
  wrong hash (a trimmed zstd extent must fail to decode, not decode to zeros).
- **H3 (outside-map blocks).** Every checksum-valid tree block that lies outside the current chunk
  map is placed, at the offset where it was scanned, by at least one historical map, and by the
  map of its own time (the newest surviving chunk root not newer than the block). Registered:
  100 % for both on the no-discard builds. A block's header carries its logical address, so this
  tests the maps against ground truth the maps were not built from.
- **H4 (the second witness).** Every stripe of every chunk in a historical map is confirmed by a
  DEV_EXTENT in some scanned dev-tree leaf, and the map assembled from DEV_EXTENTs alone agrees
  with the CHUNK_ITEM maps on every chunk both know (same logical address, length and stripe
  offsets). Registered: 100 % on the no-discard builds, and no chunk known from DEV_EXTENTs only.

**What would refute it.** A file that reads `complete` through a historical map with a hash other
than the logged one (then "every byte was read" is not enough, and the map or the rule that
picks it is wrong, or the space was overwritten without our check noticing). A valid outside-map
block that no map places (then a chunk-tree generation was lost, and H3's "own time" rule needs
a fallback that the experiment would then have to evaluate separately). A stripe that a
DEV_EXTENT contradicts.

**What it means for the paper.** H1 and H3 together are claim C6 at file level and at block
level. N-numbers in paper-draft.md that rest on "outside the current chunk map" (the 21 of 71
sandbox orphans, the 133 states of EXP-004) get their explanation: those blocks are where an
older map says they should be.

## 2. Method

**Scenario.** `corpus/vm/scenarios/s01.guest.sh`, the recipe of the corpus images
`s01_discard_{none,async,sync}_r1`: pinned `mkfs.btrfs` 6.6.3, guest kernel 7.0.0-31, 512 MiB,
`compress=zstd,commit=5`. A subvolume with `keep.txt`, `deleted_big.txt` and an inline file, their
SHA-256 logged; a read-only snapshot; the two `deleted_*` files removed; six committed one-line
writes; a full balance ("had to relocate 3 out of 3 chunks"); unmount.

**Builds.** Five fresh builds per discard mode (none, async, sync), each measured and deleted; a
guest run is not bit-stable (CONTRIBUTING.md §5). The three corpus images are measured too, as
extra lines, for the record.

**Measurement per build.** `catalog build --full-sweep`; then `recover --root all --tree all`
twice into separate directories, once with `--maps current` (M4's behaviour) and once with
`--maps own`. Artifacts are matched between the two runs by (state, tree, inode, creation
generation).
- *H1/H2:* the regular files that are `partial` with an `unmapped` range under `current`; how
  many of them are `complete` under `own`; of the `complete` ones named `keep.txt` or
  `deleted_big.txt`, how many carry the logged hash; every `complete` file under `own` whose name
  the log knows and whose hash differs is counted separately (expected 0). Files whose status is
  the same in both runs are counted as a check that the maps change nothing else.
- *H3:* from `nodes` (valid, `maps_here` 0) and `node_maps`: blocks placed by some historical map;
  blocks placed by the map of their own time. Each placement is recomputed by the script from
  `chunks` and `stripes`, not taken from `node_maps` on trust.
- *H4:* from `stripes.dev_extents` over the historical maps; and the chunk-by-chunk comparison of
  the `dev_extents` map with the CHUNK_ITEM maps.
The image's hash is taken before and after.

**Reported.** For every quantity the median and the range over the five builds of a mode, and the
host. A bound that bites (`problems`, sources `roots` and `chunk_maps`) is reported per build.
